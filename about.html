Artist Info

Bio

Erica Harris is a Brooklyn-based artist working across collage, painting, drawing, printmaking, and film. She draws inspiration from urban materials, debris, and languages of her metropolis. She also teaches art to children internationally, facilitating projects in India, Central America, Southeast Asia, Macedonia, Brazil, and New York City.

Her practice explores themes of family, school, work, play, death, violence, society, and environment through collaborative art-making with communities where English isn't primary. She collects ephemera while traveling—old encyclopedias, found photographs, sewing patterns, maps, and packaging—incorporating street discoveries into narratives addressing destruction, survival, memory, and loss.

Selected Exhibitions

Solo exhibits include venues in Brooklyn, Varanasi, Rosendale, and Monroe. Group shows span institutions including the Brooklyn Museum, St. Ann's Warehouse, and Halsey Institute of Contemporary Art (2002–2011).
